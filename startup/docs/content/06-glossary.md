# 6. Glossary {#glossary}

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Master** | A screen listing one kind of record (users, roles, places, files). |
| **Navigator** | The map of every module, report and master, showing what your role can open. |
| **Role** | A named set of permissions assigned to users. |
| **Permission** | The right to do one thing (e.g. `User.delete`). Roles are built from permissions. |
| **Layout** | A saved arrangement of a grid's columns — which show, their order and widths. Private or public. |
| **Company** | The organization your account works under. An account can be linked to more than one. |
| **Super admin** | A user who can act across companies and change system-wide roles. |
| **Session** | A signed-in account on a device. Shown as Online, Signed in or Offline. |
| **Approval** | A new account is pending until an administrator approves it; only then can it sign in. |
| **Two-factor (2FA / TOTP)** | An authenticator-app code added on top of the password. |
| **Announcement** | A banner shown across the app to the company or to chosen roles, between chosen dates. |
